1. How to Register and Log In

Tip: Multiple registration methods available. Choose the one that works best for you!

1.1 Choose Registration Method

1.2 Register Using Sign in with Google

Step: Select Sign in with Google

Open in Google Chrome

Step 1: Select the account you want to use to log in

Step 2: Click the account you want to log in with

Step 3: When you see Login successful, the login was successful

Open in Other Browsers

Step 1: Enter your Google email address and click Next

Step 2: Enter your Google email password and click Next

Step 3: When you see Login successful, the login was successful

1.3 Register Using Sign in with Email

Supported email types: QQ Mail, Gmail, 163 Mail

Step 1: Select Sign in with Email

Step 2: Click Register to create your email account

Step 3: Enter your email address

Step 4: Set your password and enter it again to confirm (make sure they match)

Step 5: Obtain your email verification code and enter it

Step 6: Click Register Now. When you see Registration successful, registration is complete and you will be logged in automatically

2. How to Earn Points

Multiple ways to earn points and get rewards!

2.1 First-Time Registration Bonus: 500 Points (Only for First-Time Users)

Please refer to How to Register and Log In?

Step 1: Go to the Rewards Page

Step 2: Click View Records to check your points details

2.2 Invite Friends: Earn 500 Points per Invitation (Unlimited)

Note: The friend must complete registration for the reward to be valid

Step 1: Go to the Rewards Page

Step 2: Click Invite, then scroll down to find your sharing link

Step 3: There are two ways to invite friends. It is recommended to use the second method (direct click)

Step 4: After your friend completes registration, check the points details to confirm the points have been credited

2.3 Watch Dramas to Earn Points (Members & Non-Members)

Points Earning Rules While Watching

Regular Users: Earn 10 points per minute

Member Users: Earn 20 points per minute (double reward)

Any video with a timer allows you to earn points

When the timer shows Claim Now, the points have been credited

3. How to Get Membership

Membership Exchange Rule: 10,000 points = 1 year membership

3.1 How to Get Membership

Step 1: Go to Profile Center and click the membership section

Step 2: Click Purchase to unlock membership

Step 3: You can check your membership expiration date here

4. How to Use Points to Redeem LOL

Exchange Rate: 100 points = 1 LOL

4.1 Points-to-LOL Exchange Entry

Step 1: Click to enter Exchange

Step 2: You can see the exchange rate: 100 points = 1 LOL

Step 3: Example conversions

  • 100 points = 1 LOL
  • 1000 points = 10 LOL
  • 10000 points = 100 LOL

Enter the number of points according to your balance

5. How to Withdraw

Withdrawal Information

MetaMask Download: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.metamask

LOL Token Address: 0xc7Ef0284223D188Ccbd71797922946ac0058dFA9

Minimum USDT Withdrawal: 20 USDT

Minimum LOL Withdrawal: 100 LOL

5.1 How to Add the LOL Token

Step 1: Open the MetaMask wallet homepage and switch to the Base network

Step 2: Click the plus icon

Step 3: Copy the contract address 0xc7Ef0284223D188Ccbd71797922946ac0058dFA9 and click Next

Step 4: Click Import

Step 5: Return to the homepage. Seeing LOL token means success

5.2 How to Find Your Receiving Address

Step 1: Click Lollipop Coin

Step 2: Click Receive

Step 3: Click Copy Address to obtain your receiving address

5.3 How to Withdraw LOL

Step 1: Go to Wallet

Step 2: Follow the steps in the image to switch to LOL

Step 3: Withdrawal request submitted successfully

Congratulations! You have completed the tutorial!
