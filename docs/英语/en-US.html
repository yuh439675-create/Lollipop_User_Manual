1. How to Register and Log In

1.1 Choose a Registration Method

1.2 Register Using Sign in with Google

  • Select Sign in with Google

Open in Google Chrome

  • Select the account you want to log in with
  • Click the account you want to use
  • When “Login successful” appears, it means you have logged in successfully

Open in Other Browsers

  • Enter your Google email address, then click Next
  • Enter your Google email password, then click Next
  • When “Login successful” appears, it means you have logged in successfully

1.3 Register Using Sign in with Email

  • Select Sign in with Email
  • Click Register to create your email account
  • Enter your email address (supported: QQ Mail, Gmail, 163 Mail)
  • Set your password and re-enter it to confirm (must match)
  • Get your email verification code
  • Enter your email verification code
  • Click Register Now
  • When “Registration Successful” appears, registration is complete and you will be logged in automatically

2. How to Earn Points

2.1 500 Points for First Registration (First-Time Only)

  • Please refer to How to Register and Log In
  • Go to the Rewards Page
  • Click View Records to see your points details

2.2 Invite Friends – 500 Points per Successful Referral (Unlimited)

(Your friend must successfully register for the reward to be valid)

  • Go to the Rewards Page
  • Click Invite, then scroll down to find your personal sharing link 🔗
  • There are two ways to invite friends (recommended: use the second method by clicking directly)
  • After your friend successfully registers, check the points details to confirm the points have been credited

2.3 Watch Dramas to Earn Points (Members & Non-Members)

Points Earning Rules

  • Regular Users: Earn 10 points per minute

  • Members: Earn double points, 20 points per minute

  • Any video with a timer allows you to earn points

  • When the timer shows “Claim Now”, the points have been credited

3. How to Get Membership

3.1 Membership Redemption Rules

10000 points = 1 year membership

  • Go to Profile and click on the membership section
  • Click Purchase to unlock membership
  • You can check your membership expiration date here

4. How to Redeem Points for LOL

4.1 Points-to-LOL Exchange Entry

Exchange Rate: 100 points = 1 LOL

  • Click Redeem
  • View the exchange rate: 100 points = 1 LOL

  • Examples:

    • 100 points = 1 LOL
    • 1000 points = 10 LOL
    • 10000 points = 100 LOL
  • Enter the number of points you want to exchange based on your available balance

5. How to Withdraw

MetaMask Download: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.metamask

LOL Token Contract Address: 0xc7Ef0284223D188Ccbd71797922946ac0058dFA9

Minimum USDT Withdrawal: 20 USDT Minimum LOL Withdrawal: 100 LOL

5.1 How to Add the LOL Token

Step 1: Open your MetaMask wallet homepage and switch to the Base Network

Step 2: Click the ➕ button

Step 3: Copy and paste 0xc7Ef0284223D188Ccbd71797922946ac0058dFA9 Then click Next

Step 4: Click Import

Step 5: Return to the homepage. If you see the token displayed, it means the import was successful.

5.2 How to Find Your Receiving Address

Open your MetaMask wallet, make sure you are on the Base Network, and copy your wallet address from the homepage. This is your receiving address.

5.3 How to Withdraw USDT

  • Enter the withdrawal page in the APP
  • Select USDT
  • Enter your receiving wallet address
  • Enter the withdrawal amount (minimum 20 USDT)
  • Confirm the withdrawal

5.4 How to Withdraw LOL

  • Enter the withdrawal page in the APP
  • Select LOL
  • Enter your receiving wallet address
  • Enter the withdrawal amount (minimum 100 LOL)
  • Confirm the withdrawal
